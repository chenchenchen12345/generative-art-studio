Resonance Field

A Chladni plate, made of sound instead of metal: thousands of grains hunt for the standing-wave nodes of a vibrating surface, the same way sand collects into geometric figures on a real plate driven by a tone generator. The pitch of your voice or instrument bends which pattern the plate wants to form; louder input shakes the grains loose, and they resettle the instant it's quiet again.

This needs mic access to analyze sound. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — analysis happens live in your browser.
