Cellular Automata

A Conway-style cellular automaton, kept alive and reshaped by your microphone in real time. Bass hits inject new live cells like a beat triggering birth, treble shifts the hue, mid brightens it, and volume controls how fast the automaton steps forward.

This needs mic access to analyze sound. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — analysis happens live in your browser.
